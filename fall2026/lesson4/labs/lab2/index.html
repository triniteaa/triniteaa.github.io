Welcome to Wild Rescues

Welcome to Wild Rescues. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and release wildlife back into their natural environment. We are a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping injured wildlife.

Our staff provides around-the-clock care for wildlife in need. We also provide long-term care for wildlife that cannot be released back into their natural environment.

Will you partner with us? We need caring volunteers to help care for our animals. You can also donate, become a member, or become a sponsor. Contact us today.

Latest Rescues

The following are recent animals brought into our care that are currently undergoing rehabilitation.

Baby Fawns
Fawns require specialized formula feedings every few hours and secure outdoor enclosures to practice running and building muscle strength before release.
Red-Tailed Hawk
Birds of prey are monitored in large flight cages to evaluate wing healing, flight stability, and hunting readiness.
Orphaned Raccoons
Young raccoons are housed in social groups to develop critical foraging skills, climbing agility, and healthy wild behaviors.
